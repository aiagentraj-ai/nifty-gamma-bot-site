Privacy Policy

nifty gamma bot is used only by its owner.

  • Google Drive access: the app uses the limited drive.file permission. It can only see, create and change files that it created itself in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot read any other files.
  • What is stored: only the owner's own market-data recordings, uploaded by the owner's own server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.
  • Personal data: the app does not collect personal information from anyone.
  • Removal: the owner can revoke the app's access at any time in their Google Account settings.
